💡 Common Chatbot Weaknesses

  • Hallucination: Inventing products, prices, or features that don't exist
  • Over-promising: Agreeing to discounts, free items, or services not offered
  • Context Confusion: Losing track of what was discussed earlier
  • Prompt Injection: Being tricked by "ignore previous instructions"
  • Language Mixing: Responding in wrong language or mixing languages
  • Number Errors: Wrong product numbers, prices, or calculations
  • Competitor Mentions: Badmouthing or comparing with competitors
  • Sensitive Data: Revealing business metrics or customer info
  • Off-topic: Being led into unrelated conversations
  • Dialect Issues: Not understanding local expressions or slang
